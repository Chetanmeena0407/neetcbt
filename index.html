GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

Please read the instructions carefully

General Instructions:

  1. The total duration of the examination is 03:00:00 (3 Hours).
  2. The timer will appear at the top right corner.
  3. Read every question carefully before answering.
  4. Do not refresh or close the browser during examination.
  5. Your responses will be automatically saved.
  6. The Question Palette shows the status of each question.

Navigating to a Question

  1. Click on a question number in the Question Palette.
  2. Click Save & Next to save your answer.
  3. Click Mark for Review if required.

Answering a Question

  1. Select one option.
  2. Click Clear Response to remove answer.
  3. Save & Next to continue.

Navigating through Sections

  1. You can switch between sections.
  2. Question palette will indicate question status.
